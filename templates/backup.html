Backup & Restore

Encrypted full-instance backup

Includes CA private keys, certificates, profiles, users, HTTPS identity, SCEP journal/history and trash. Changes on both listeners pause briefly during the snapshot, then resume automatically.

Choose a unique password of at least 12 bytes (maximum 1024). A long random passphrase is recommended. The password is never stored. If lost, the backup cannot be recovered. Existing CA passphrases are still required after restore.

Replace this instance

Restore replaces all logical data, including users and trash. Your current account may disappear. Browser sessions and every unused SCEP challenge are invalidated. Used enrollment history and certificates are retained.

The archive is validated and staged before activation. Successful restore shuts down NetAnchor: containers with a restart policy restart automatically; standalone binaries must be started again. Sign in with a restored account after restart.

Restoring an older snapshot loses later issuance records; certificates already distributed remain valid. Keep the source stopped when moving an instance. Deployment configuration and external unlock secrets are not included.
