What a certificate profile determines

A profile sets the certificate purpose and permitted issuers. The requester's private key stays with them; this profile and the CSR determine the subject and key.

{{end}}

Paste a PEM-encoded CSR or upload a file. The requester keeps their own private key.

Generating a test CSR

Generate a CSR with OpenSSL:

openssl req -newkey rsa:2048 -nodes -keyout test.key -out test.csr -subj "/CN=test.local"
