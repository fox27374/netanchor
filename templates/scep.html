Endpoints and interoperability

Endpoint: /scep (also /scep/pkiclient.exe, as Cisco IOS-XE requests it, and /cgi-bin/pkiclient.exe). The optional HTTP listener serves only SCEP.

Create a dedicated leaf-only RSA SCEP intermediate on the CA page first. RSA keys only (no ECDSA yet), AES-128-CBC, AES-256-CBC or 3DES-CBC (single DES only when allowed above), and SHA-256, SHA-384 or SHA-512. Cisco interoperability is unverified.

Signing CA

Only dedicated SCEP intermediates whose keys are unlocked are listed. Encrypted CAs unlock at startup from the passphrase files in NETANCHOR_CA_SECRETS_DIR.

Challenge rules

One device: issued SANs are exactly those authorized here, even if absent from the CSR. Extra CSR SANs are rejected. The challenge works once; same-transaction retries are answered for seven days.

Shared: the source address must be inside a listed range and becomes the only IP SAN. Names are accepted only under a listed suffix (a label boundary, so evil-example.net does not match example.net) and only if they resolve to the source address or name an inventory device at it. Residual risk: anyone holding the secret on a device inside a listed range can obtain a certificate for that device's own address.

Certificate days are capped by the template and by the issuer's expiry.

Revoking a challenge does not revoke certificates already issued. Revoked or expired challenges can be deleted; issued certificates stay.
